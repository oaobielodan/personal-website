How might we desgin messaging to be eyes-free?

Overview
Eyes-Free Messaging was a user study designed to explore non-visual communication techniques in mobile messaging. As touchscreens become increasingly central to daily interaction, accessibility and usability for eyes-free contexts (such as while walking, multitasking, or for users with visual impairments) remain critical challenges. This study focuses on how users can receive and send emoticons without relying on visual or auditory cues, using only haptic feedback and gesture-based input. The primary objective of this research was to identify which haptic and gesture methods were most effective and intuitive for eyes-free communication, offering insights into designing more inclusive and accessible mobile interaction systems.
